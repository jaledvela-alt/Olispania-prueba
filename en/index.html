Welcome

Two families,
one table.

Olispania Aceitunas, S.L. was founded in 2012 when two families with more than 30 years of experience in the sector joined forces, with one main goal: to make a product of the highest quality.

Our philosophy is also focused on excellence in food safety and customer satisfaction. That is why we have held IFS certification at higher level since 2017 (96.35 out of 100).

We are especially proud of our wide range of fillings, including anchovy, red pepper, lemon, salmon, prawn, chorizo, ham and garlic.

We also offer a selection of dressings and flavours made with the Manzanilla, Verdial, Gordal, Cacereña and Cuquillo varieties. Together with our pickles, they make up our 2026 catalogue.

We work with food service and with large retailers in Spain and abroad, under our own brands (Olispania and Vitaloliva) and as private label, in glass jars and cans.
